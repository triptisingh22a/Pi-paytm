# Assumptions — all shop data is simulated

- No real Paytm access. A simulated adapter provides amounts (A) or item data (B). POS availability does not establish Paytm access to costs, inventory, cash, customers or all payment channels. Those fields require merchant-authorized data.
- Fixed synthetic reference date 2026-10-02; 365 history days; seed 42. Festival dates are configurable synthetic event dates, not an authoritative calendar.
- Four personas and 200 background shops. Unit costs, demand, uplift, lead times, cash, dues and interest are planted assumptions, not estimates of Paytm merchants.
- Tier A cash forecasts require explicitly supplied cash balance and obligations. Payments alone cannot identify cash runway, category demand, margins or full sales.
- Demand: Poisson GLM-like ridge fit on log(count+0.5), weekday, payday and event indicators; censored stock-out days excluded. Predictive Poisson uncertainty omits coefficient uncertainty; show this caveat.
- Promotion: matched weekday/event/payday comparison; randomized synthetic control series with planted uplift. Causal identification is only defensible under the generator's assumptions. Pull-forward is estimated from post-period matched controls. Live merchant causal claims require a controlled test.
- Cash: 2,048 seeded Monte Carlo paths, independently sampled daily demand; settlements at T+1 or T+2. Opening receivables and all recorded liabilities included; unrecorded expenses invalidate results.
- Borrowing cost default 2% over the comparison horizon is an illustration only, not a rate or offer. Nudges require B, a projected cash gap, positive incremental restock return exceeding cost and explicit consent. No application is submitted.
- Newsvendor opportunities use a finite scenario distribution; integer quantities solved as multiple-choice knapsack via scipy MILP. Greedy return-per-rupee is not exact for heterogeneous indivisible costs.
- Cash holding has zero incremental profit and undefined ROI; it is ranked as a risk safeguard, not divided by zero.
- Cached AI uses deterministic templates and local routing, not a recording of a live LLM. No fabricated wrapper duel.
- The Sweet-shop persona has an illustrative INR 3,800 opening supplier due, creating a known cash gap for the warning demo. This is planted, not observed.
- UI primitives use Radix/shadcn-style components with custom CSS and Tailwind; count forecasting uses the documented numpy/scipy equivalent rather than statsforecast.
- Partner credentials are absent. Unverified partner APIs remain disabled mocks. Browser speech is optional, permission-dependent and may use vendor cloud services.
- Docker is unavailable in the build environment; Compose cannot be runtime-verified here.

## Revised billing-first standalone demo (2026-10-02)

The current frontend is a full website at `/demo`, reached from the fullscreen landing page. The previous phone frame is removed. Legacy financial modules and their four datasets remain under Decision lab and the original API; their numbers are not presented as observations of the new billing merchants.

- Seven focal shops have 365 days of synthetic bills, line items, stock receipts and per-bill availability logs, 2025-10-03 through 2026-10-02. The snapshot is fixed, not today's real date. Day is the snapshot day; week and month are trailing 7 and 30 days, not calendar periods.
- Hypermarket, restaurant and apparel are primary demos. Kirana, pharmacy, bakery and electronics add frequency, identity, shelf-life and ticket-scale diversity. Pharmacy replacements are retail fixture trials, not medical substitutions or suitability advice.
- There are 40 synthetic donors per business type. Donors store weekly sufficient statistics of generated bill opportunities; full raw donor invoices are not stored. Never describe donor facts as real transactions from other merchants.
- Prices and landed ingredient/item costs are supplied fixture inputs. Gross contribution is revenue less units-sold costs; it is not net profit. Costs, recipe quantities, facilities and demographics are never transferred from peers.
- Stock balance is initial receipts minus recorded units sold, plus confirmed local imports. Current replacement trials use no replenishment, expiry, salvage or disposal. The legacy stock simulator separately retains FIFO, expiry and lead-time examples.
- Restock nudges use current on-hand / latest 7-day unit velocity, with a 7-day coverage threshold. This is an assumed lead-time/buffer check, not an optimal purchase order. Rate-change alerts are descriptive; no significance or causal trend claim is made.
- A product with zero units and positive available-bill exposure is labeled “no recorded sales,” not zero latent demand. Not-stocked candidates have no exposure and cannot be called dead.
- Synthetic loyalty identifiers are present with probability 0.65 per bill. “Returning” counts bills whose recorded ID appeared before the selected window. Anonymous bills and first-seen loyalty IDs do not establish distinct new people. Profiles are the largest-value basket category, not inferred age/gender/income; completed bills do not count all visitors.
- Exact fixture IDs/aliases preserve pack, unit and variant. No production GTIN parser, fuzzy matcher or embeddings are claimed. Ambiguous/unrecognized descriptions abstain. Identity, family and substitutes are separate concepts.
- Similarity uses only non-candidate category shares, with extra weight on the replacement family, within the same business type. Candidate and removed-item outcomes are excluded. This demo exact-reranks 40 shops; scalable ANN retrieval remains a production design, not implemented infrastructure.
- Eligible donors need at least 200 known available-bill opportunities. Select at most 30; fewer than 10 abstain. ESS is shown separately. Peer rates are weighted aggregate units / weighted exposure. The empirical prior is capped at 500 opportunities; the target update uses its latest 28 days. Peer weeks are complete bins within the historical window; no future weeks enter.
- Customer flow is exogenous and fixed: completed bills in the latest 30 days divided by 30, multiplied by trial days. It is a conditional bill-opportunity proxy, not a recovered arrival rate. Nonbuyers, assortment-induced arrivals and acquisition/retention effects are not identified here.
- Gamma-Poisson posterior rate draws become conditional MNL-like utilities by dividing each rate by max(1 − summed current-family rates clipped to [0.01,0.8], 0.2). This explicit calibration is an assumption because baskets can contain more than one item. Outside weight defaults to 1 with 0.5/1/2 sensitivity. IIA, one family choice per opportunity and peer exchangeability remain limitations.
- Baseline and scenario share rate draws and uniform choices. Depleted items leave the choice set. Existing removed stock is set aside; new trial stock is purchased once. Other categories cancel by assumption. Incremental contribution deducts costs of units sold; cash deducts the upfront candidate purchase, so costs are not double-counted.
- Restaurants additionally consume supplied shared recipe quantities and stop selling a dish when a required ingredient is unavailable. The ingredient snapshot is allocated to the modeled family; other-category consumption and future deliveries are not simulated. Prepared-dish stock imports do not replenish ingredients.
- P10/P50/P90 and loss probability describe the chosen simulator, seed and assumptions. Outside sensitivity is a set of alternate models, not confidence bounds. Synthetic holdout coverage is not real-world calibration. A controlled small assortment trial is needed for causal uplift.
- The default agent is a constrained local router with deterministic, input-bound templates. Optional Gemini chooses one validated allowlisted tool; model prose and model arithmetic never enter the response. Without Gemini, exact product aliases and supported intents are needed; broad conversational understanding is not claimed.
- Voice uses separately enabled Sarvam STT/TTS, never auto TTS after chat. Hindi/Marathi recordings request STT translation for the English local tool router; transcripts are reviewed before sending. Native microphone/code-mixed accuracy is not established by a synthetic audio roundtrip.
- Supplier-bill preview is a labeled cached extraction sample with editable fields. Confirmation saves stock arrivals atomically, is idempotent by merchant+invoice, and rejects payload collisions. Actual image OCR is not connected.
